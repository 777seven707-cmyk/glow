Grief & loss

For a person, a version of you, or a future you'd planned on.

There's a pain that the right words can't fix.

When someone or something is gone, you sometimes hear "time heals."

But time doesn't bring people back.

It doesn't return your old life.

It doesn't give you back the chance to say what you didn't get to say.

So it can feel unfair when people expect you to just "move on."

You might not be ready.

You might miss them.

You might be angry.

You might feel guilty.

You might feel relief, and then feel ashamed of that relief.

You might laugh one day and cry the next.

Grief doesn't owe anyone logic.

It comes in waves.

Sometimes it feels like you're finally breathing more freely, and then some completely small detail pulls you right back to that day.

A song.

A smell.

A photo.

A place.

A phrase.

And it hurts again.

That doesn't mean you're back at the beginning.

It means you had something worth losing.

You don't have to forget someone to keep living.

You don't have to stop missing them.

You don't have to turn your pain into a tidy story about how "everything happens for a reason."

Sometimes things happen that have no good explanation.

And you're allowed to say:

"I don't understand why this happened."

You're allowed to be angry.

Allowed to not have an answer.

Allowed to miss them for as long as you need to.

But please don't let your grief convince you that your life ended along with what you lost.

It changed.

That's not the same thing.

You may now have to build a new version of your life — not the one you had pictured before.

And that can take a long time.

There's no need to rush.

You don't need to know today what your life will look like in five years.

Sometimes it's enough to know what to do in the next ten minutes.

Drink some water.

Sit down.

Breathe.

Call someone.

Cry.

Sleep.

Wake up.

And take the next small step.

You can keep loving what you lost while slowly learning to keep living.

Continuing to live is not a betrayal of the past.

Your memories will stay part of you.

But you're still allowed to laugh.

Allowed to love.

Allowed to make plans.

Allowed to feel joy again, someday.

And if that feels impossible right now, that's okay.

You don't have to force yourself to believe in a happy future today.

Just leave it room to arrive, someday.

If this feels bigger than a website can hold, that's okay — that's what Support, below, is for.
