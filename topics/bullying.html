Bullying & harassment

Online, at school, at work — none of it is your fault.

If you're being bullied, humiliated, mocked, harassed, or deliberately made to feel worthless, the first thing you need to hear is:

you don't deserve this.

It doesn't matter how different you are from other people.

Doesn't matter how you look.

Doesn't matter how you talk.

Doesn't matter what you wear.

Doesn't matter how many friends you have.

Doesn't matter whether you've made mistakes.

None of that gives another person the right to humiliate you.

Bullying works by slowly wearing down your sense of your own worth.

First you hear other people's words.

Then you start remembering them when you're home alone.

Then you look in the mirror and start saying to yourself what they used to say to you.

And at some point, it can start to feel like the problem really is you.

Stop. Step back and look at this from the outside.

If someone is constantly told they're worthless, that doesn't mean they actually are.

It means they've had cruelty repeated at them for too long.

You don't have to believe the voices of people who are trying to break you.

If this is happening to you right now, please don't stay in it alone.

Keep messages, screenshots, photos, and any other evidence. Tell someone you trust. If the first adult doesn't help, tell a second one. If the second one doesn't help, keep looking for someone who takes it seriously.

You shouldn't have to earn the right to be safe.

If there are physical threats, stalking, or immediate danger, get to a safe place and seek urgent help.

And one more thing.

Please don't let the people who hurt you write the rest of your story.

They only know a small part of you.

They don't know who you're going to become.

Don't know how many people you'll still meet.

Don't know what you'll go on to build.

Don't know who you'll be in a few years.

What's happening right now is part of your story. It is not the whole story.

You don't have to become strong right this moment.

For now, it's enough to just get through this period.

Then to heal.

Then to slowly learn to trust again.

And one day you may look back and realize that the people who once tried to decide your worth never actually had that power.

Your worth was yours from the very beginning.

If this feels bigger than a website can hold, that's okay — that's what Support, below, is for.
